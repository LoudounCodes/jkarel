The next step after Karel

Familiar actions.
More possibilities.

Moving, turning, sensing, and collecting beepers remain the foundation. Students use those familiar actions to explore colored teams, doors that open, persistent players, and scoreboards that react to events.

The API gives the world a clearer vocabulary: robots have locations in an Arena, directions are enum values, and Pacing controls how actions unfold. Interfaces and subclasses enter the lesson when a project needs them.
